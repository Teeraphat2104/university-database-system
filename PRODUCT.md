# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University staff and administrators who manage the institutional document archive. Their primary tasks are uploading PDFs, organizing documents into categories, curating metadata, and overseeing user accounts. Public visitors browse and search the archive to find documents.

## Product Purpose

A centralized document management system for a university archive. Staff upload, categorize, and manage PDF documents year by year. Public visitors search and browse the collection without an account. Success means documents are findable, well-organized, and accessible to anyone who needs them.

## Brand Personality

Clean, institutional, professional. Calm and trustworthy — like a university library reading room. Not a startup, not a SaaS platform. The interface should feel durable and matter-of-fact, not trendy or promotional.

## Anti-references

Overdesigned corporate dashboards with heavy gradients, rainbow-colored stat cards, excessive shadow depth, and SaaS marketing cliches. Avoid anything that would look out of place in a university IT system: glassmorphism, gradient text, side-stripe borders, or sketchy illustrations. The design should not compete with the content.

## Design Principles

1. **Content is the interface.** Documents and their metadata are the reason this exists. Every layout decision prioritizes scanability, searchability, and readability over decoration.

2. **Professional calm.** The interface stays out of the way. Low visual noise, predictable layouts, restrained color use. Quiet confidence rather than visual excitement.

3. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA minimum. High contrast, readable type, clear focus indicators, semantic HTML. No motion that can't be reduced. No information conveyed by color alone.

4. **Institutional durability.** The design should feel like it has been there for years and will be there for years more. No trendy micro-interactions, no ephemeral design fashions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Sufficient color contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text). Reduced motion support for all animations. Keyboard-navigable interface. Screen reader friendly labels and semantics.
